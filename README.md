# RPCMMS v3 – Rig & Plant Maintenance Management System

RPCMMS (Rig & Plant Maintenance Management System) is a **SaaS-first, industry-focused CMMS** designed specifically for **oil & gas, drilling rigs, and heavy industrial operations**.

It is built to replace complex, expensive, consultant-driven CMMS platforms with a practical, asset-centric system that reflects real-world maintenance workflows.

---

## Why RPCMMS Exists

Most CMMS platforms on the market today are:
- Expensive to license and implement
- Overly complex and poorly adopted by technicians
- Built for generic factories, not harsh industrial or offshore environments
- Dependent on consultants for even small configuration changes

RPCMMS is being built by a **maintenance Subject Matter Expert with real offshore and industrial experience** to solve these problems directly.

The goal is simple:
> **Enterprise-grade capability, without enterprise-grade waste.**

---

## Core Principles

- **Built by operations, not just developers**
- **Asset-first design** (not ticket-first)
- **High usability for technicians and supervisors**
- **Safety, compliance, and traceability built-in**
- **Lower total cost of ownership by design**

---

## Key Capabilities (High Level)

- **True Asset Hierarchy**  
  Plant → Area → System → Equipment → Component → Part

- **Work Requests & Work Orders**  
  Industry-standard workflows with built-in fault-finding logic (5-Why, Fishbone)

- **Planned Maintenance (PM)**  
  OEM-aligned PMs with calendar and hour-meter scheduling and optimisation tracking

- **Inventory & Spare Parts Management**  
  Asset-linked BOMs, part number control, multi-location stock, and traceability to work orders

- **Safety & Compliance**  
  LOTO, permits, SIMOPS awareness, and job-level tool & equipment planning

## SaaS-First Delivery Model

RPCMMS is designed primarily as a **cloud-based Software-as-a-Service (SaaS)** platform.

### What this means:
- No on-premise infrastructure required for customers
- Secure web-based access
- Automatic updates and improvements
- Rapid onboarding and scalability
- Predictable subscription-based pricing

The SaaS model significantly reduces deployment time and total cost of ownership compared to traditional enterprise CMMS platforms.

---

## Optional On-Premise / Dedicated Server Deployment

While SaaS-first, RPCMMS also supports **dedicated server or private-cloud deployment** for clients with strict security, data residency, or regulatory requirements.

This option is commonly required in:
- Oil & gas operators
- Government or state-linked entities
- Regulated or high-security environments

In these cases:
- RPCMMS is deployed on customer-owned infrastructure or a private cloud
- The customer receives a fully isolated system instance
- Commercial terms follow a hybrid model (setup + annual support)

This flexibility allows RPCMMS to compete effectively with legacy enterprise CMMS solutions while remaining modern and cost-efficient.
