# RPCMMS v3 – Asset Model & Hierarchy

This document describes the **asset model used in RPCMMS v3**.

The asset model is the foundation of the platform and directly influences:
- Maintenance history and traceability
- Planned maintenance effectiveness
- Inventory and spare parts accuracy
- Failure analysis and optimisation

RPCMMS is designed around how assets actually exist and fail in
**oil & gas, drilling, and heavy industrial operations**.

---

## Why the Asset Model Matters

In many CMMS platforms, assets are treated as flat or loosely structured records.

This leads to:
- Poor failure tracking
- Inaccurate maintenance history
- Weak PM optimisation
- Disconnected spare parts data

RPCMMS addresses this by enforcing a **clear, multi-level asset hierarchy** that mirrors real-world physical systems.
